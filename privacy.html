Privacy policy

This policy covers gws CLI (personal), a private command-line set-up used by one person with their own Google account. Last updated: 30 October 2026.

Who uses it

Only the owner of the Google account it connects to. It is not offered to anyone else.

What it accesses

With the owner's consent, it reads and changes the owner's own Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Sheets and Docs, and only when the owner runs a command. A background task refreshes the sign-in a few times a day so that it keeps working; that refresh reads no email, files or other content.

Where the data goes

Data stays on the owner's computer. Sign-in tokens are stored locally on that computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or sent to analytics services.

Google API Services

The use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from the Google Account's third-party connections page.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the support email shown on the app's Google sign-in screen.
